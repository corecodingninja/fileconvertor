## 🚨 Common SEO Mistakes to Avoid

- ❌ Don't keyword stuff (maintain 1-2% density)
- ❌ Don't create duplicate content across tools
- ❌ Don't hide text with same color as background
- ❌ Don't use excessive redirects
- ❌ Don't ignore mobile users
- ❌ Don't have slow page load times
- ❌ Don't block CSS/JavaScript from crawlers
- ❌ Don't create thin/low-value content
- ❌ Don't ignore Core Web Vitals
- ❌ Don't neglect internal linking
