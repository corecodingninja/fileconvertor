About ConverterHub

ConverterHub is a privacy-first utility dedicated to providing high-quality file conversion tools for free, without the need for accounts or intrusive tracking.

Our Mission

In a world where online tools often harvest user data, ConverterHub was built on a different principle: Privacy by Default. We believe that your documents are private, and changing their format shouldn't mean giving up your data.

The Technology

Our conversion engine is powered by LibreOffice, the world's most powerful open-source office suite. By running LibreOffice in a "headless" server environment, we can provide high-fidelity conversions that rival expensive desktop software, right in your browser.

How We Handle Your Files

  • Local Processing: Files are processed on our secure servers and never shared with third parties.
  • Auto-Deletion: To ensure maximum security, every file uploaded or converted is automatically purged from our servers after 10 minutes.
  • No Accounts: We don't store your personal information because we don't ask for it. No signup, no email, no password.
