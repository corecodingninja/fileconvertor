Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 3, 2026

At ConverterHub, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you visit or use our services.

1. The Data We Collect

Unlike most online converters, we do not require registration or personal information (like your name or email) to use our tools. We collect minimal data necessary to provide and improve the service:

  • Uploaded Files: Files you upload are stored temporarily on our secure server for the sole purpose of conversion.
  • Usage Statistics: We collect anonymized usage data, such as file sizes and conversion times.
  • IP Addresses: We do not store plain IP addresses. All IP addresses are SHA-256 hashed before storage for security and rate-limiting purposes, making them impossible to trace back to you.

2. Data Retention Policy

Your files are automatically deleted 10 minutes after upload or conversion. We do not keep backups or copies of your documents. Once a file is deleted, it is gone forever and cannot be recovered by us or anyone else.

3. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies to maintain your session and ensure the converter functions correctly. We may also use third-party analytics (like Google Analytics) or advertising partners (like Google AdSense) who may use cookies to serve ads based on your visit to this and other websites.

4. Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. Data Security

We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your files while they are on our servers. This includes encryption in transit (SSL/TLS) and secure server environments.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time in order to reflect changes to our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons.

7. Contact Us

For more information about our privacy practices, please contact us at privacy@converterhub.com (placeholder).
